=== SLIDE 2 ===
The Problem: Does a Client Really Need to Be Unlearned?
Core Concepts
• Federated Learning FL Trains a shared global 
model using decentralized contributions across multiple 
clients.
• Deletion Requests: A client may later request 
complete deletion or "unlearning" of their contribution 
(e.g., under GDPR.
• Client-level DP Mathematically bounds how much 
any individual client's contribution can influence the 
final model during training.
Unlearning & Overhead
• Explicit Unlearning: Attempts to actively remove or 
suppress that client's historical influence post-training.
• Trade-offs & Costs: Explicit unlearning incurs 
substantial additional computation, communication 
rounds, and historical update/data storage overhead.
Main Research Question: "Does explicit unlearning provide enough additional privacy benefit to justify its operational 
and computational cost?"

Mechanistic Explanations
Gradient Ascent explicitly maximizes loss on forgotten 
client data to erase learned patterns.
Negative-Gradient Unlearning applies opposing 
gradient steps to subtract the target client's 
contribution.
Both methods may successfully reduce 
forgotten-client accuracy below baseline 
expectations.
Trade-offs & Privacy Dynamics
Aggressive model updates risk damaging overall 
global utility and retained-client accuracy.
PRIVACY WARNING
A lower forgotten-client accuracy score alone does not 
guarantee robust privacy protection against membership 
inference attacks.

Cryptographic Secure Aggregation: Implementation & Architectural Theorem
Simulating Zero-Sum Blinding Masks and Proving the Impossibility of FedEraser
Pairwise Zero-Sum Masking Protocol
• Threat Model: Semi-honest central server; individual updates (Δ_i) 
remain unobserved.
• Masking Mechanism: Clients blind deltas with pairwise random 
vectors that sum to zero.
• Exact Aggregation: Masked updates cancel out perfectly (∑ Δ̃_i = 
∑ Δ_i).
• Zero Knowledge: Server computes global update without storing 
client deltas.
Architectural Theorem: FedEraser Fails Under SecAgg
• Core Incompatibility: FedEraser relies on unencrypted client 
update caches.
• Implementation Verdict: Raises 
IncompatibleSecurityRegimeError; FedEraser is BLOCKED.
• Key Takeaway: Client-Level DP M_DP & Retraining M_R) are 
the only viable paradigms.
